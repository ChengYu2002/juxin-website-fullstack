# Commercial Policy

## KB-COMMERCIAL-MOQ

**Buyer questions**

- What is your MOQ?
- Why do different models have different MOQs?

**Approved answer**

The exact MOQ for a listed product must be retrieved from the product database. [待填写：补充一般 MOQ 原则、定制对 MOQ 的影响和可协商边界。]

**Must not claim**

- 不得用 RAG 中的旧 MOQ 覆盖 Product Tool 返回值。

## KB-COMMERCIAL-MIXED-ORDER

**Buyer questions**

- Can I mix different models or colors in one order?
- Can I place a trial order?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：混款、混色、试单、整柜/拼箱的真实政策和条件。]

## KB-COMMERCIAL-QUOTATION

**Buyer questions**

- What affects the price?
- How long is a quotation valid?
- Can you quote in USD or EUR?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：型号、数量、材料、定制、包装、贸易条款、汇率等报价因素和支持币种。]

**Must not claim**

- 不得在 Agent 中给出未由业务员确认的价格、折扣或报价有效期。

## KB-COMMERCIAL-PAYMENT

**Buyer questions**

- Which payment methods do you accept?
- What are your deposit and balance terms?
- Do you accept letters of credit?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：可以公开的付款方式和一般流程。具体条款变化时必须人工确认。]

## KB-COMMERCIAL-DISCOUNT

**Buyer questions**

- Do you offer volume discounts?
- Can you give me your best price?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：只解释影响因素和报价流程，不写未经确认的折扣。]

**Escalation**

收集型号、数量、目的地、定制、包装和目标时间后转业务员报价。

## KB-COMMERCIAL-DISTRIBUTOR

**Buyer questions**

- Can I become your distributor?
- Do you offer exclusive agency rights?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：经销、区域合作和独家代理的初步资格与评估流程。]

**Must not claim**

- 不得自动授予区域、价格保护或独家权利。
