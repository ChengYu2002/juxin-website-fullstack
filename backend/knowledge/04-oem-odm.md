# OEM and ODM Capabilities

## KB-OEM-LOGO

**Buyer questions**

- Can you print our logo?
- Which logo methods do you support?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：丝印、刺绣、标签、铭牌、纸箱印刷等真实工艺及适用产品。]

**Conditions / exceptions**

- Logo file format:
- Printing position:
- Color limit:
- Plate/setup fee:
- MOQ dependency:

**Information to collect**

- Product model
- Quantity
- Vector logo file
- Position and size
- Number of colors

**Must not claim**

- 不得在未看设计稿和产品结构时承诺某种工艺一定可行。

## KB-OEM-COLOR

**Buyer questions**

- Can you customize the color?
- Do you accept Pantone colors?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：标准色、定制色、Pantone/RAL/实物色样、色差和 MOQ 规则。]

## KB-OEM-PACKAGING

**Buyer questions**

- Can you customize the carton or retail packaging?
- Can you add our barcode and instructions?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：纸箱、彩盒、说明书、标签、条码、运输唛头的能力。]

**Information to collect**

- Artwork files
- Required languages
- Barcode standard
- Retail/channel requirements
- Quantity

## KB-OEM-PRODUCT-MODIFICATION

**Buyer questions**

- Can you change the wheels, handle, dimensions or material?
- Can you modify an existing model?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：允许评估的轻度 OEM 项目，以及必须进入工程评审的结构修改。]

**Must not claim**

- 未经工程评审不得承诺承重、尺寸、材料或安全性能变化。

## KB-ODM-NEW-DESIGN

**Buyer questions**

- Can you develop a new model from our drawing?
- Do you provide ODM service?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：需求评审、图纸、原型、模具、测试、确认和量产流程。]

**Information to collect**

- Drawings or reference images
- Intended use
- Target specifications
- Compliance market
- Forecast quantity
- Target launch date

## KB-ODM-TOOLING-OWNERSHIP

**Approved answer**

[待填写：模具费、所有权、保管、维护、修改和停止合作后的处理原则。]

## KB-ODM-CONFIDENTIALITY

**Approved answer**

[待填写：NDA、图纸访问、知识产权和保密资料处理原则。]

## KB-OEM-COST-TIME-ESCALATION

**Approved answer**

Customization cost, MOQ and lead time depend on the product, design and order quantity and must be confirmed by the sales and engineering teams.

**Escalation**

已有真实邮箱时生成 LeadCard，并在 summary 中包含产品、数量、定制项、目的地和目标时间；没有邮箱时自然询问邮箱。
