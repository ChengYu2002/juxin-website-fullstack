# Quality Control

## KB-QC-INCOMING

**Buyer questions**

- How do you inspect incoming materials?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：原材料、外购件、轮子、金属件、面料等来料检查项目和记录方式。]

## KB-QC-IN-PROCESS

**Buyer questions**

- What checks are performed during production?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：焊接、表面处理、装配、折叠机构、紧固件和关键尺寸的过程检查。]

## KB-QC-FINAL

**Buyer questions**

- How do you inspect finished products?
- Do you test load capacity?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：外观、功能、承重、轮子、折叠、包装、标签和数量检查。]

**Conditions / exceptions**

- [待填写：哪些测试适用于哪些产品类别]
- [待填写：抽检还是全检]

## KB-QC-AQL

**Buyer questions**

- Which AQL standard do you use?
- Can you inspect to our standard?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：真实 AQL 标准、抽样方式；没有统一标准时写按订单和买家要求确认。]

## KB-QC-REPORT-TRACEABILITY

**Buyer questions**

- Can you provide an inspection report?
- Can you trace a production batch?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：报告、批次、照片、记录保存和可追溯范围。]

## KB-QC-THIRD-PARTY

**Buyer questions**

- Can SGS or another inspection company inspect the order?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：第三方验货支持、预约时间、地点、费用和放行条件。]

## KB-QC-NONCONFORMING

**Approved answer**

[待填写：不合格品隔离、返工、复检、报废和纠正措施流程。]

## KB-QC-MUST-NOT-CLAIM

**Must not claim**

- Zero defects
- Every product passes every possible international standard
- A test applies to products outside the documented scope
- Third-party certification when only internal testing exists
