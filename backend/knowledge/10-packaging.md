# Packaging

## KB-PACKAGING-STANDARD

**Buyer questions**

- What is your standard packaging?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：按产品类别说明塑料袋、纸箱、泡沫、护角、托盘等真实标准包装。]

**Conditions / exceptions**

- 单品装箱量必须由 `getProduct` 查询，不在此处复制。

## KB-PACKAGING-CUSTOM

**Buyer questions**

- Can you customize the carton or retail box?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：普通纸箱、彩盒、展示包装、材料和印刷能力。]

## KB-PACKAGING-LABEL

**Buyer questions**

- Can you add barcodes, carton marks or multilingual labels?
- Can you prepare Amazon/FBA labels?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：条码、运输唛头、警示标签、说明书、多语言和渠道标签能力。]

## KB-PACKAGING-ARTWORK

**Approved answer**

[待填写：包装稿格式、刀模、校对、颜色、签样和最终批准流程。]

**Information to collect**

- Product/model

- Required languages
- Barcode/label standard

## KB-PACKAGING-COST-TIME

**Approved answer**

Customized packaging may affect MOQ, cost and lead time. The sales team must confirm these details after reviewing the design and order quantity.

**Must not claim**

- 不得在未审稿时承诺印刷效果、成本或完成时间。
