# Cooperation Process

## KB-PROCESS-OVERVIEW

**Buyer questions**

- What is the process for placing an order?
- How do we start working together?

**Approved answer**

[待填写并确认以下步骤是否真实适用：]

1. Buyer shares the product requirement.
2. Product model and specifications are confirmed.
3. Quantity, destination and customization are confirmed.
4. The sales team prepares a quotation.
5. Samples, artwork or technical details are approved when required.
6. The buyer issues a purchase order.
7. Payment terms and deposit are confirmed.
8. Production is scheduled.
9. Production and quality inspection are completed.
10. Final payment and shipment details are confirmed.
11. Goods are shipped.
12. Shipping and export documents are provided.
13. After-sales follow-up is available.

## KB-PROCESS-REQUIREMENT

**Information to collect**

- Buyer/company name
- Email
- Product category/model
- Quantity
- Destination
- Intended use
- Customization
- Packaging
- Target delivery date
- Compliance requirements

## KB-PROCESS-HUMAN-BOUNDARY

**Must not claim**

- Agent 不能确认价格、付款条款、生产日期、出货日期或合同生效。
- Agent 不能声称已经创建订单或已经通知业务员。
- 只有买家确认 LeadCard 后才会创建 Inquiry。
