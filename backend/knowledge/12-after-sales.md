# After-Sales and Claims

## KB-AFTERSALES-WARRANTY

**Buyer questions**

- Do your products have a warranty?
- What does the warranty cover?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：正式保修期限、覆盖范围、起算点和排除项；没有统一政策时转人工。]

**Must not claim**

- Lifetime warranty
- Coverage outside the written policy
- Automatic refund or replacement

## KB-AFTERSALES-DAMAGE

**Buyer questions**

- What should I do if products arrive damaged?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：运输破损报告时限、外箱和产品照片、视频、数量和批次信息。]

## KB-AFTERSALES-QUALITY-CLAIM

**Buyer questions**

- How do you handle a quality problem?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：投诉登记、证据收集、批次追溯、原因分析、责任判断和回复流程。]

**Information to collect**

- Order/PO number
- Product/model
- Affected quantity
- Batch/carton information
- Photos and videos
- Description of use and failure
- Delivery date
- Buyer contact email

## KB-AFTERSALES-RESOLUTION

**Buyer questions**

- Will you replace, refund or provide spare parts?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：可用处理方式和批准条件；强调具体方案需调查后确认。]

## KB-AFTERSALES-SHORTAGE

**Approved answer**

[待填写：数量短缺、错发、包装缺失的证据与处理流程。]

## KB-AFTERSALES-ESCALATION

**Escalation**

售后赔偿和责任认定必须人工处理。已有邮箱时生成 LeadCard，summary 中包含订单号、型号、数量、问题和证据情况。
