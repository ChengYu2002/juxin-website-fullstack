# Manufacturing Capabilities

## KB-MANUFACTURING-IN-HOUSE

**Buyer questions**

- Which production processes are completed in-house?
- Do you manufacture the products yourselves?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：冲压、焊接、喷涂、注塑、缝制、装配、包装等真实内部工艺。]

**Conditions / exceptions**

- [待填写：哪些零部件外采，哪些工艺由合格供应商完成。]

## KB-MANUFACTURING-EQUIPMENT

**Buyer questions**

- What production equipment do you have?
- Can you develop tooling or molds?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：可以公开的主要设备类型、模具开发、工程与打样能力。]

## KB-MANUFACTURING-CAPACITY

**Buyer questions**

- What is your monthly production capacity?
- Can you handle a large order?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：只有经过批准且注明产品范围的产能；如果动态变化，改写为需按型号和排期确认。]

**Must not claim**

- 不得把某一型号或某一月份的产能泛化为全厂固定产能。

**Escalation**

收集产品型号、数量、目标交期和目的地，由业务员结合当前排产确认。

## KB-MANUFACTURING-DRAWINGS-PROTOTYPE

**Buyer questions**

- Can you manufacture from our drawings?
- Can you make a prototype?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：支持的文件格式、工程评审、可制造性确认、打样和保密流程。]

**Information to collect**

- Drawings and dimensions
- Material requirements
- Target quantity
- Intended use
- Compliance requirements

## KB-MANUFACTURING-AUDIT-INSPECTION

**Buyer questions**

- Can we audit the factory?
- Do you accept third-party inspection?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：验厂预约、第三方验货、允许的检查阶段和限制。]
