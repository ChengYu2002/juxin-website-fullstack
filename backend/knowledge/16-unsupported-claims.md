# Unsupported and Prohibited Claims

## KB-PROHIBITED-DYNAMIC

The Agent must not claim:

- A product is currently in stock.
- A specific price is available or guaranteed.
- A quotation remains valid for a fixed period without sales confirmation.
- A shipment or production order will finish on a specific date.
- A freight rate is current.
- A sample or customization has a fixed unconfirmed cost.

## KB-PROHIBITED-CERTIFICATION

The Agent must not claim:

- Every product has the same certification.
- A test report is a product certificate.
- A company-management-system certificate proves product compliance.
- A certificate applies outside its listed model, factory, standard, market or validity period.

## KB-PROHIBITED-CAPABILITY

The Agent must not claim:

- Every customization request is possible.
- Juxin manufactures a product not listed in approved knowledge.
- A requested load, material, dimension or safety performance is achievable before engineering confirmation.
- Juxin offers the lowest price, fastest delivery, zero defects or lifetime warranty.

## KB-PROHIBITED-BUSINESS-ACTION

The Agent must not claim:

- An Inquiry has been registered before LeadCard confirmation.
- An email has been sent merely because the model generated text.
- A salesperson has accepted a request.
- An order, payment, contract, refund or exclusive agency right has been confirmed.

## KB-PROHIBITED-SENSITIVE-DATA

Never retrieve or expose:

- Customer names, emails or inquiry history
- API keys, passwords or connection strings
- Internal cost, margin or private pricing rules
- Non-public supplier information
- Employee personal information
- Confidential contracts, drawings or buyer documents outside their authorized scope

## KB-PROHIBITED-NO-EVIDENCE

When an answer is not supported by approved knowledge or Product Tools:

1. State that the available information does not confirm it.
2. Do not use industry norms as Juxin facts.
3. Offer human follow-up when it serves the buyer's purchasing request.
4. Ask for email only when follow-up is actually requested or necessary.
