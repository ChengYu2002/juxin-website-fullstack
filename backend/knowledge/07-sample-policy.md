# Sample Policy

## KB-SAMPLE-AVAILABILITY

**Buyer questions**

- Can I order a sample?
- Which products are available as samples?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：样品资格、适用产品、标准样和定制样区别。]

## KB-SAMPLE-COST

**Buyer questions**

- Is the sample free?
- Can the sample fee be refunded?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：只写稳定政策；具体金额必须转人工报价。]

**Must not claim**

- 不得在没有当前报价时给出固定样品费。
- 不得承诺样品费一定抵扣，除非正式政策如此。

## KB-SAMPLE-SHIPPING

**Buyer questions**

- Who pays the sample freight?
- Can I use my courier account?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：运费承担、快递账号、承运方式和清关责任。]

## KB-SAMPLE-CUSTOMIZATION

**Buyer questions**

- Can the sample include our logo or custom color?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：定制样品范围、需要的文件、费用和额外确认。]

## KB-SAMPLE-PREPARATION

**Buyer questions**

- How long does it take to prepare a sample?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：如准备时间动态变化，说明取决于库存、型号和定制要求，必须人工确认。]

## KB-SAMPLE-INFORMATION

**Information to collect**

- Product/model
- Quantity
- Standard or customized sample
- Customization requirements
- Destination and postal code
- Courier account, if any
- Buyer email

**Escalation**

样品属于人工跟进事项。已有邮箱时生成 LeadCard；没有邮箱时询问邮箱。
