# Production and Lead-Time Process

## KB-PRODUCTION-START-CONDITION

**Buyer questions**

- When does production start?
- What must be confirmed before production?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：PO、定金、样品、规格、包装稿和其他开工条件。]

## KB-PRODUCTION-LEAD-TIME

**Buyer questions**

- What is the production lead time?
- Can you deliver by a specific date?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：说明交期取决于型号、数量、定制和排产；只有正式批准的范围才可写区间。]

**Must not claim**

- 不得承诺具体交货日期。
- 不得把历史订单周期当成当前订单承诺。

**Escalation**

收集型号、数量、定制、目的地和目标到货时间，由业务员结合当前排产确认。

## KB-PRODUCTION-CUSTOM-ORDER

**Approved answer**

[待填写：定制、模具、测试和包装确认为什么可能延长周期。]

## KB-PRODUCTION-PEAK-RUSH

**Buyer questions**

- Can you handle a rush order?
- Does peak season affect production?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：旺季和加急订单的评估原则，不做自动承诺。]

## KB-PRODUCTION-PROGRESS

**Buyer questions**

- Will you provide production updates?
- Can you send production photos?

**Approved answer**

[待填写：进度更新、照片、里程碑和联系人机制。]

## KB-PRODUCTION-DELAY

**Approved answer**

[待填写：发生变化时的通知、重新确认和升级流程。]
