## KB-LOGISTICS-FREIGHT

**Buyer questions**

- How much is shipping?
- What is the current freight rate?

**Approved answer**

Freight depends on the destination, shipment volume, transport method and current carrier rates and must be confirmed by the sales or logistics team.

**Information to collect**

- Product/model and quantity
- Destination country, city and postal code
- Preferred transport method
- Required delivery date
- Trade term

**Must not claim**

- 不得从旧文档或历史订单复用运费。

**Escalation**

已有邮箱时生成 LeadCard；没有邮箱时询问邮箱。
